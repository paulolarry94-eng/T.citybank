How it was tested

Dukascopy 1-minute bid data in UTC, sessions in local time with daylight saving. Signals on completed 5-minute bars; entries at the next minute's open paying the spread, commission and slippage; pending orders filled minute by minute; a stop and target in the same minute count as the stop; gaps fill at the worse price; one trade per session, closed by the session end. Chronological split: development 2011–2019, validation 2020–2022, final 2023–2025, with the final years masked from every search. The full report, code and every experiment are in the repository under research/orb.
