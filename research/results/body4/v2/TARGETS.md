# Body-engulf range: 4R vs 2R vs 1R (12 markets pooled)

Entry 50%, stop at the range end, lookback on; breakeven at 50% of the target ON (owner's main) and OFF. 2011-19 (2020-22 in brackets). 'Needed' = the win rate that breaks even after costs, about (1 + cost) / (target + 1); it is exact only with breakeven OFF (every trade is then a full win or a full loss, apart from Friday closes). With breakeven ON many trades end near 0 (minus commission), so their win rate looks lower.
