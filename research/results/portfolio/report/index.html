Why a few big winners do not make a profitable year

With TP2 as the only target, a winning trade paid —R on average, but only — trades in 10 won and each of the others lost 1R. Per 10 trades that came to —R. The big winners on a chart are real, but so are the losers around them, and a rule earns on all of its trades, not on the ones that are shown.

Method, data, costs and what is not yet proven
  • Data. One-minute bid bars from 2011 to 2025; EURGBP, NAS100 and the other HistData markets were clock-corrected for daylight saving. Jan-Oct 2026 comes from Exness's public tick archive. All times are UTC.
  • Costs. Older legs: Exness Zero, with spreads measured from Exness's tick archive and commission taken at the high end of published rates. v2 legs: RoboForex Prime published spreads and commission. Slippage is added on stops. Overnight swaps are charged for every night a trade is held. The stress profile multiplies costs by 1.5 and swaps by 2.
  • Sizing. Fixed fraction: each trade risks the base risk times its leg's weight, of current equity, compounding daily. Profit or loss is booked on the exit day. The base risk was set on 2011-22 so that 95% of bootstrapped 36-fortnight lifecycles stay within the envelope.
  • Periods. 2011-22 is in-sample: the legs, weights and base risk were chosen on it. 2023-25 had been looked at for every leg before the mix was built. Jan-Oct 2026 was never used to set a rule or a weight of the older legs; the v2 legs were kept after a look that included it.
  • How hard the search was. The legs are survivors of — configurations across — ideas tested in this project; the v2 legs were picked from — cells. A heavily searched backtest usually runs 30 to 50% weaker live, so treat the in-sample figures as an upper bound.
  • Statistics. Sharpe and Sortino from weekly returns times the square root of 52; drawdowns on daily equity; Calmar = return a year ÷ worst drawdown; correlations on daily results.
  • Not yet proven. There is no live or demo record. Most legs have no Expert Advisor yet (TimelessCity covers the gold S1 leg; the rest exist as research code). The account's real costs and swap status need exporting from the account and the mix re-scored before money is risked, followed by 3 to 6 months on demo.
